What the words mean

Every term the tracker uses · all times are Pacific
Reading a runner
LegOne of the 36 relay segments (L1–L36). Each person runs several.
Van 1 · Van 2The two squads of six, shown in blue and purple. They leapfrog, one resting while the other runs.
StartWhen a leg begins — the time the runner took over from the leg before.
On the roadThe runner is out on their leg right now.
On legHow long the current runner has been out on this leg.
PaceMinutes per mile for a leg.
Total timeHow long a leg takes, start to finish.
Times and the plan
ETAThe estimated finish time of a leg — when that runner reaches the next exchange. Re-figured from the times actually recorded so far, so it sharpens as the race runs.
PlannedThe schedule from the team sheet — what was expected before anyone ran.
Est.Marks a leg that hasn't run yet: its start and finish are estimates, not recorded times.
Exchange · handoffWhere one runner passes to the next (Sandy, OMSI, …). The van exchanges are where Van 1 and Van 2 trade off.
Adjust race startCorrects the recorded race start time. Every ETA re-bases from it, so only change it if the start time is wrong.
Top bar
Elapsed · Until startThe big clock: counts down to the start, then counts up from it.
FinishThe projected time the whole relay reaches Seaside. Tap it to see how far ahead of or behind plan the team is.
The dotSync status. Green = everything's saved to the sheet. Amber = sending, or changes are queued. Red = offline. A number is how many changes are still waiting.
↻ RefreshPulls the latest from the sheet right now, instead of waiting for the once-a-minute check.
Roadkills
Roadkills 🏃🦞Runners from other teams you pass on a leg. Tap the icon on a finished leg to log them. The count earns a Halo medal — Killing Spree at 5, Killing Frenzy at 10, Running Riot at 15, all the way up.
